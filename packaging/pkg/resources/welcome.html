pressy

Brings the old Pressy headphone-jack button back to life on your Mac. Press patterns like ., .. or - run Spotify, Raycast, Apple Shortcuts, scripts, key combinations and more.

This installs, for the current user:

  • ~/.local/bin/pressy, the daemon (starts at login)
  • ~/Applications/Pressy.app, the menu bar app with the configuration editor
  • ~/.config/pressy/config.yaml, only if you have no config yet

Needs an Apple silicon Mac with a headphone jack, macOS 14 or newer.
