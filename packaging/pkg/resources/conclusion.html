Almost there

  1. Plug the Pressy into the headphone jack.
  2. When macOS asks, allow Input Monitoring for pressy (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring). Without it the button stays silent.
  3. Press once: Spotify plays. Click the ◎ in the menu bar to see every press and edit your patterns.
